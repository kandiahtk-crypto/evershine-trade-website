Privacy & GDPR Statement

UK Inbound Ground Transport Operations (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information We Collect

We collect business-related contact information from tour operators, destination management companies and trade partners including:

  • Company name and contact details
  • Operations or contracting contact information
  • Passenger numbers and itinerary details for transport planning
  • Email correspondence relating to programme delivery

We do not collect unnecessary personal data. Passenger-level data is only requested where operationally required (e.g. group size or special mobility requirements).

How We Use Information

Data is used solely for:

  • Preparing quotations and contracts
  • Operational planning and delivery of UK touring programmes
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory transport requirements
  • Communication with trade partners

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely using password-protected systems. Access is restricted to authorised personnel involved in operational delivery.

Data Retention

Commercial and operational records are retained only for as long as required for accounting, regulatory or contractual obligations.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure where legally applicable
  • Object to processing where appropriate
