Modern Slavery & Human Trafficking Statement

This statement is made in accordance with Section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and outlines the steps taken by UK Inbound Ground Transport Operations to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking within our business and supply chains.

Our Business

We provide UK inbound ground transport services to international tour operators and destination management companies, supporting multi-day touring programmes and fixed departure series.

Our Commitment

We have a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and are committed to operating responsibly and ethically in all business dealings.

Supply Chain Oversight

We work with licensed UK coach operators and transport providers who are required to:

  • Comply with UK employment law
  • Operate legally licensed vehicles
  • Maintain lawful working hours and conditions
  • Provide compliant driver contracts

Due Diligence

We assess suppliers based on regulatory compliance, operator licensing and industry standards.

Responsibility

Senior management holds responsibility for ensuring compliance with this statement and reviewing supplier relationships.

This statement is reviewed annually.
