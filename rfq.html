Programme Intake Requirements

To ensure structured fleet allocation and operational feasibility, please provide the following information for confirmed departure programmes.

  • Confirmed departure dates
  • Full itinerary with hotel list
  • Passenger numbers & luggage profile
  • Arrival & departure gateway
  • Special routing requirements (e.g. Highlands, Ireland)

All submissions undergo structured operational review prior to fleet allocation confirmation.
