Fleet, Compliance & Risk Management

We operate within a structured compliance framework designed to meet the expectations of international tour operators, DMCs and procurement teams. Our operational model prioritises safety, regulatory compliance and consistent programme delivery.

Operator Licensing & Regulatory Compliance

  • Vehicles operated under valid UK Operator Licences
  • Compliance with UK & EU Drivers’ Hours Regulations
  • Tachograph monitoring and working time compliance
  • Regular vehicle safety inspections (DVSA standards)
  • Full adherence to UK road transport legislation

Insurance Coverage

All vehicles and services are covered by:

  • Comprehensive Motor Insurance
  • Public Liability Insurance
  • Employers’ Liability Insurance

Certificates of insurance available upon request.

Driver Standards

  • Valid CPC certification
  • Category D / PCV licensed drivers
  • Ongoing professional training
  • Familiarity with UK touring routes and city access regulations

Operational Risk Management

Each touring programme is reviewed for:

  • Daily mileage feasibility
  • Driver rest requirements
  • Urban access restrictions (e.g. London ULEZ, city coach bans)
  • Airport timing constraints
  • Seasonal congestion planning

Supplier Oversight

Where additional fleet support is required, we engage only licensed and compliant UK operators who meet defined operational and insurance standards.

Health & Safety

  • Vehicle maintenance schedules documented
  • Emergency escalation procedures in place
  • 24/7 operational contact availability during active programmes

Documentation & Audit Readiness

Compliance documentation, insurance certificates and operator licence details are available to contracting and procurement teams upon request.

We understand the due diligence requirements of international travel companies and operate accordingly.
