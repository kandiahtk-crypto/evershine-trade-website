Procurement, Compliance & Risk Framework

Structured compliance oversight, operational governance and documented risk controls supporting international tour operators, DMCs and procurement teams operating UK touring programmes.

Regulatory Compliance

  • Valid UK Operator Licence compliance
  • Full adherence to UK & EU Drivers’ Hours Regulations
  • Tachograph monitoring & working time compliance
  • DVSA-aligned vehicle safety inspection standards
  • Ongoing regulatory monitoring and internal oversight

Insurance & Liability Coverage

  • Comprehensive Motor Insurance
  • Public Liability Insurance
  • Employers’ Liability Insurance
  • Documentation available upon formal request

Driver Governance Standards

  • Category D / PCV licensed drivers
  • Valid CPC certification
  • Professional conduct and safety training
  • Operational familiarity with UK touring routes & city regulations

Operational Risk Management

Each confirmed touring programme is reviewed prior to allocation to ensure structured operational feasibility and regulatory compliance.

  • Daily mileage feasibility review
  • Driver rest & rotation compliance
  • Urban access controls (ULEZ, congestion zones)
  • Airport & cruise terminal timing analysis
  • Peak-season contingency planning

Supplier Oversight & Fleet Control

Where additional fleet capacity is required, engagement is restricted to licensed UK operators meeting defined compliance, insurance and operational standards.
